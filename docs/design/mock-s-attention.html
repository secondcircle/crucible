Needs you — the third row state

A row already says two things at once: viewing is the slab and the weight, working is the counter and the three green dots, the only motion in the rail. This adds a third: the turn ended in a session you were not looking at. It cannot be motion (that means working) and it cannot be the slab (that means viewing), so it lands in the right block, which is already the row's status column, and it lifts the title out of dim into full ink so the cue is not color alone.

The Tab walk

Three sessions finish while you are in Sidebar working-state visual language. Tab takes the topmost one asking, in rail order, crossing into the next workspace when this one is clear. Landing on a row clears it, whether or not you type anything.

The workspace roll-up, beside the board count

A collapsed or scrolled-past workspace needs the count on its own row. The board's existing need you badge is already there and means something else entirely — branches and pull requests, not sessions. Filled pill for sessions, outlined pill for the board, sessions first.

Quota strip rounds the wrong way past $10

Workspace and state on line one, session title on line two. Clicking it opens that session. It plays macOS's default notification sound, first banner in a burst only, so four sessions finishing together are four banners and one chime.

What I'd build: variant 1.

  • An amber pip in the right block, above the time, plus the title in full ink and the time in amber. The pip is static; nothing but working animates.
  • Variant 2's amber left bar reads as a second kind of selection and fights the orange slab on a rail where several rows are asking at once. Variant 3's done word is clearer at a glance but costs the "4m ago" that tells you how long it has been sitting there.
