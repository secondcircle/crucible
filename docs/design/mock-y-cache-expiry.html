Every idle expiry, no dollar floor. Idle expiry is rare enough that the interruption is always worth it, and the person deciding wants the facts rather than a threshold guessing on their behalf. A two-cent break still shows its two cents. Deliberate breaks stay silent: a model switch is one keystroke old and telling you about it turns Shift-Tab into a two-step gesture. The orchestrator row is the honest gap in this feature: the sessions that idle longest are the ones a run wakes, and a run's message cannot stop to ask anybody. Those keep taking the miss until the hour covers them, which is what the retention change is for.
