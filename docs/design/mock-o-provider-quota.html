The pace arithmetic, in one line

Elapsed fraction of the window comes from the reset instant and the window's own length (7D and FABLE are seven days). Projected finish is used ÷ elapsed. No history, no sampling, no stored series — the same rough estimate you would do in your head, done for you. It says nothing at all in the first day of a window, where the divisor is small enough to produce nonsense.
